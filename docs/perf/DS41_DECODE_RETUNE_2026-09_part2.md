### A1, the arena edge on MinHeap: measured, the recipe stays at 100 GiB

`ATLAS_DS41_EXPERT_CACHE_GIB` parsed as an integer and fell back to 88 GiB on "100.5" without a word; it now parses as f64 (5d029914c). Three standards with the trace on and a 1 Hz memory watch beside the serve (`ds41_memwatch.sh`: MemAvailable, swap used, `pswpout` / `pswpin`, PSI memory):

| arena | slots | MinHeap (r1 / r2 / r3) | Volvo (r1 / r2 / r3) | MinHeap misses a step r2 / r3 | MinHeap r1 TTFT | swap during the serve | PSI peak |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 100 GiB | 8,381 | 21.34 (13.69 / 21.34 / 21.87) | 23.87 (15.40 / 23.87 / 23.90) | 2.20 / 1.24 | 4,481 ms | none (+3 pages) | 0.00 |
| 100.5 GiB | 8,423 | 21.57 (13.68 / 21.57 / 22.12) | 23.84 (15.39 / 23.84 / 23.90) | 1.77 / 0.72 | 4,474 ms | 311 MiB out at the arena allocation, 13 MiB in r2 | 3.02 |
| 101 GiB | 8,465 | 21.32 (11.05 / 21.32 / 22.49) | 23.76 (15.38 / 23.87 / 23.76) | 1.59 / 0.46 | 84,743 ms | 4.6 GB out, 10.4 GB in (load + r1 prefill) | 98.9 |

The slot count is not what MinHeap's residual is made of: r3 goes 1.24 -> 0.72 -> 0.46 misses a step, but r2 (the median) 2.20 -> 1.77 -> 1.59, because r2's prefill re-misses 386 / 357 keys that r1's decode evicted (the LRU's request cycle, what the phase 6 Belady simulation found); even at 8,465 slots r1 evicts 228. The whole edge is worth ~0.4 ms of a 46 ms step on the median. And `MemAvailable` ~8.4 GiB beside a 100 GiB arena is not free margin: 100.5 swaps 311 MiB at the allocation, 101 thrashes through the load and the first prefill. The real headroom above 100 GiB is under 512 MiB; by the rule (no swap growth on either suite) the recipe stays at 100. Memory map at 100 GiB: arena 100 GiB device + 196 MiB pinned ring, off the ledger; GPU ledger 7.29 GB = raw Q2_K / Q3_K residents 2,591 MB + Q2_K-to-bf16 expansions 2,056 MB (the token embedding 129,280 x 5,120 bf16 = 1,324 MB, Q2_K in the file; the engram `wkv` bf16 2 x 315 MB, the prefill GEMM's operand) + KV 1,286 MB (2,057 blocks) + the Q6_K head 517 MB + ~530 MB of buffers; buffer arena 290 MB; inference reserve 512 MB; serve RSS ~0.9 GiB; other processes ~0.8 GiB. The bf16 embedding table could go by construction (a gather that dequantises the token's row from the raw Q2_K blocks, -1.1 GB): not built, because 1.1 GB makes 100.5 safe but not 101, and the gain on this box is inside the standard's noise; on the B200 it is real memory.

### A2, the quantisation launches folded (5c6d392ca): KEPT

The M4b profile had 395 `kquant_q8_1_rows_bf16` launches a token, ten a layer: four in the attention (each on the row the previous kernel produced: no fold without touching the producers) and six in the MoE, where the shared expert quantised the token's row twice more after the routed experts had, and both SwiGLU outputs went through a separate launch.
