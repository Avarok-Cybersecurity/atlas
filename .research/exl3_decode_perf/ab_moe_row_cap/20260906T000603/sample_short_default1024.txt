Let me think through the design:

1. **Arena pattern**: Instead of using `Box<Node>` or `Rc<RefCell<Node>>`, we store all nodes in a `Vec<Node>` and use indices (usize) to link them. This avoids heap allocation per node and gives better cache locality.

2. **Doubly linked list**: We need prev/next pointers (as indices) so we can:
   - Move a node to the front on access (O(1) with prev/next)
   - Remove the tail (least recently used) in O(1)
