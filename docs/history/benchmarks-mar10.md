## Key Observations

1. **35B is the fastest model** at 133 tok/s (conc=1, sTPS) with MTP K=2 speculative decoding
2. **MTP K=2** provides ~30-35% speedup over baseline on supported models (35B, 80B)
3. **Concurrency scaling** is sub-linear — aggregate Tput stays roughly constant as per-request TPS drops
4. **TTFT scales linearly with ISL** as expected (chunked prefill with 1024-token chunks)
5. **27B Dense is bandwidth-bound** — 14 tok/s is 76% of the 17.6 tok/s hardware ceiling
6. **122B EP=2** limited to ISL≤512; ISL=1024+ triggers CUDA 700 on worker during chunked prefill
7. **Nemotron-H has high TTFT** (1s at ISL=128, 26s at ISL=4096) due to 52-layer dense prefill
